Privacy policy

The Desk Crew: healthy desk habits · Last updated November 24, 2026

The Desk Crew collects no personal data. Everything it needs stays in your own browser, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the extension keeps, and where

WhatWhyWhere
Your settings: working hours, which reminders are on, times, water goalSo reminders come when you choseChrome’s extension storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome copies your settings to your other computers through your own Google account.
Reminder history: which reminder came, when, and whether you did it, snoozed or skipped it; glasses of water loggedThe counts in the toolbar and your weekly statsOnly on this computer, in Chrome’s extension storage. The latest 2,000 entries are kept.

What it checks, and never keeps

  • Video calls. It checks whether a tab is open on a call page (Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Whereby, Slack or Discord), so a reminder never appears on a shared screen.
  • Presenting or watching. If you allow reminders on web pages, it checks the page you’re looking at for slides being presented or a large video playing, so it can stay quiet.
  • Daylight. It reads your computer’s time zone to estimate sunrise and sunset, so “Step outside” only comes while it’s light out. Your location is never asked for.
  • Away from the computer. It asks Chrome whether you’ve used the mouse or keyboard in the last 20 minutes. It never sees what you type or click.

These checks happen on your computer at the moment they’re needed. Page addresses and page content are not stored, logged or sent anywhere.

What it never does

  • No accounts, sign-ins, analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.
  • No servers: the extension never sends your data over the internet.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of it for anything other than your reminders.
  • No code loaded from the internet: everything runs from the extension itself.

Your choices

  • Export your stats as a spreadsheet from Settings → Your data.
  • Reset all stats there at any time.
  • Turn off “Show reminders on the page” in Settings to stop the page checks for presenting and watching.
  • Uninstall the extension and Chrome deletes everything it stored. When you uninstall, Chrome opens a short goodbye page that asks for feedback; no data is sent with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the new version ships with an update to the extension.
