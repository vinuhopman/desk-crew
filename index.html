The Desk Crew: healthy desk habits

A free Chrome extension. Three friendly pets remind you to move, stretch, rest your eyes and drink water while you work, and never during calls or presentations.

  • Short breaks at a gentle rhythm you choose, one at a time
  • Every reminder explains why it helps, with its research source
  • Quiet during video calls, presentations, when you’re away and outside your working hours
  • No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays in your browser
